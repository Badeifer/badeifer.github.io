8-Bit Soundboard

Sound pack, icons and board In development

17 retro sound effects made from scratch, with a matching pixel-art key for each and a board ready to play.

What it does

  • Coin, jump, power-up, level up, laser, explosion, hit, game over, victory, alarm, countdown, boing, drum roll, rimshot, sad trombone, air horn and applause.
  • Every sound is synthesized here from square, triangle and noise waves, the way old game chips did it. No samples, so they are safe to play on stream.
  • All sounds levelled to the same loudness, so no key is a surprise.
  • The 8-Bit Soundboard board: fifteen of them on keys, sounds packed inside. Import and press.

You need

  • Elgato Stream Deck app 7 or later
  • Any Stream Deck; the board is laid out for 15 keys

Set it up

In development. Coming to Elgato Marketplace.

  1. Double-click the .streamDeckProfile file and pick your Stream Deck. The sounds come with it.
  2. For the icons on their own, double-click the .streamDeckIconPack file.
