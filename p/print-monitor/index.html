Print Monitor

iCUE widget In development

Your Bambu Lab printer on the Edge: live camera, progress, time left, temperatures and AMS filament.

What it does

  • The printer camera, live. Tap it to fill the widget.
  • Progress ring with the layer count, and the time left with the clock time it finishes.
  • Nozzle and bed temperatures against their targets; amber while heating.
  • Every AMS slot with its colour, type and how much is left; the loaded one ringed.
  • Speed mode, part fan and chamber light at a glance.
  • Read-only and local: it talks to the printer on your network, never through the cloud, and never sends it a command.

You need

  • Corsair Xeneon Edge
  • iCUE 5.52 or later
  • Windows
  • A Bambu Lab printer (P1, X1 or A1 series) on the same network
  • The printer's IP address, serial number and LAN access code

Set it up

  1. On the printer's screen, open Settings, WLAN and note its IP address and LAN access code.
  2. Find the printer's serial number in Bambu Studio under Device, or on the label on the back of the printer.
  3. Open %LOCALAPPDATA%\ModShop\printer.json on your PC (it appears after the widget first runs) and fill in those three values. They never leave your PC.
  4. Open the widgets section and press the plus button.
  5. Pick the .icuewidget file you downloaded, or install it straight from Elgato Marketplace inside iCUE once it is listed there.
  6. Drag the widget into a slot. Hold it to open its settings: colours, transparency and what it shows.

Permissions

iCUE asks you to approve these when you install. Here is what each one is for. Everything stays on this PC unless a line says otherwise.

Local helper app localhost:13509
Talks to a small helper app on this PC. It answers only widgets on this PC and never opens anything to the internet. Why: Holds the connection to your printer on your home network (status on port 8883, camera on port 6000) and hands the widget its status and newest camera frame. iCUE widgets cannot open those connections themselves. Swipe between Edge pages even when your finger starts on this widget. iCUE alone only swipes from the gaps between widgets.
Open links iCUE URL plugin
Opens a link or an app when you tap. Why: Starts the helper app if it is not running, so the widget fixes itself.
